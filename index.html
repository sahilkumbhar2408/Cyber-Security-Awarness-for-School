Common Cyber Threats

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails and websites trying to steal your login information. Always check sender addresses and hover over links before clicking.

Tip: Never click suspicious links. Verify before sharing info.

Weak Passwords

Simple passwords are easily guessed. Use 12+ characters with letters, numbers, and symbols.

Tip: Use a password manager and enable 2FA everywhere.

Malware

Virus, ransomware, and spyware that infects devices. Download only from trusted sources.

Tip: Keep antivirus updated and avoid pirated software.

Social Engineering

Trickery to manipulate you into giving information. Attackers pretend to be trustworthy.

Tip: Verify identities through multiple channels.

Public WiFi Risks

Unsecured networks expose your data. Hackers can intercept traffic on open networks.

Tip: Use VPN on public WiFi and avoid sensitive transactions.

Cloud Security

Misconfigured cloud accounts expose data. School Google Drives often have weak sharing settings.

Tip: Review sharing permissions regularly.
